AI integration portfolio

Research-driven AI integration that augments human labor.

I study how AI systems enter real work: how they change evaluation, accountability, job security, decision-making, safety workflows, and creative production. My focus is practical integration that improves human work rather than obscuring or replacing it.

Integration Framework

Human role clarity

  • What work remains human-owned?
  • Where does AI assist, recommend, or act?
  • How do teams assign credit and responsibility?

Evaluation and risk

  • How does AI change performance evaluation?
  • Does the system create job security concerns?
  • What assumptions shape trust or overreliance?

Adoption and design

  • Where do workflows need transparency?
  • Which handoffs need guardrails?
  • What interface cues help people use AI well?

Selected AI Integration Casework

Workplace AI | Labor augmentation

AI-enabled workplace evaluation and job security

Tested how AI design, worker presentation, dyadic partner type, and task type shape evaluations of human workers.

  • Evidence: 703 recruited, 685 valid responses, 16 conditions, SEM, expert interviews.
  • Outcome: frameworks for evaluation risk, credit attribution, and job-security concerns during AI rollout.
AI governance | Professional practice

Accountability for AI bias mitigation

Interviewed AI professionals to understand why technical ability alone does not produce accountable AI practice.

  • Evidence: 20 AI professionals across development roles and industries.
  • Outcome: organizational and workflow factors that shape whether AI teams can own and mitigate risk.
Decision support | Dashboard UX

AI-augmented readiness dashboards

Worked on dashboard concepts for high-stakes readiness assessment, focusing on cognitive load, shared responsibility, and human oversight.

  • Evidence: stakeholder-driven HCI design work and published decision-making insights.
  • Outcome: design guidance for AI-supported dashboards where speed, accuracy, and accountability matter.
Platform safety | AI moderation

AI moderation and companion support in virtual communities

Studied how users expect AI moderation and AI companions to support safety without disrupting social interaction.

  • Evidence: 39 interviews on AI moderation and 20 interviews on AI companion support.
  • Outcome: guidance for transparency, escalation, user control, and human review.
Generative AI | Creative labor

GenAI in nonprofit indie game development

Analyzed how creative communities perceive generative AI as a production tool, including where it supports work and where it conflicts with values.

  • Evidence: 1,540 online posts/comments from creative community forums.
  • Outcome: adoption insights for integrating GenAI without flattening craft, ownership, or community norms.
AI practitioner research | Design practice

AI design decisions and workplace dynamics

Interviewed AI practitioners to understand how workplace dynamics shape design decisions and responsible design practices.

  • Evidence: eight AI practitioners interviewed about design and development practices.
  • Outcome: design implications for AI teams building systems that affect workers and organizational evaluation.
