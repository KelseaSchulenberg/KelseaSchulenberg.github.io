Selected Research Casework

AI work, evaluation, and governance

Workplace AI evaluation and job security

Tested how AI design, worker presentation, partner type, and task type shape evaluation, credit, accountability, and job-security perceptions.

Evidence: 685 valid experiment respondents, 16 between-subjects conditions, 6 expert interviews.

  • Methods: factorial experiment, SEM, interviews, survey measures.
  • Use cases: AI governance, HR tech, workforce transformation, internal AI adoption.
Trust, safety, and social VR

Platform safety in embodied online spaces

Studied harassment, consent, user visibility, AI moderation, and AI companions in social VR.

Evidence: 223 global survey respondents, 39 moderation/consent interviews, 31 interviews with users from marginalized populations.

  • Methods: surveys, interviews, thematic analysis, safety-design synthesis.
  • Use cases: platform safety, gaming, online communities, AI-assisted moderation.
Emerging tech communities

Adoption, creator work, and collaboration

Mapped how people use new tools for collaboration, social support, streaming, creator work, and game development.

Evidence: 30 VR collaboration interviews, 29 marginalized population support interviews, 1,540 GenAI forum posts/comments.

  • Methods: interviews, online community analysis, cross-case synthesis.
  • Use cases: creator tools, community products, AI workflows, distributed collaboration.
Media effects and audience insight

Risk, responsibility, belonging, and support

Analyzed how news, podcasts, and online communities shape interpretation, connection, and social support.

Evidence: 181 coded news articles, 18 focus group participants, 4 independent coders.

  • Methods: quantitative content analysis, focus groups, survey intake.
  • Use cases: communications research, content strategy, public trust, audience insight.
