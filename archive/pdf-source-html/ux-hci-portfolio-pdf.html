Selected UX/HCI Projects

Dashboard UX | Decision support

Navy Squadron Readiness Dashboards

Redesigned readiness dashboard concepts for high-stakes assessment contexts where officers needed clearer status signals, responsibility boundaries, and reduced cognitive load.

  • Design focus: information hierarchy, trust calibration, review workflows.
  • Outcome: published insights on shared responsibility in high-stakes decision making.
Trust & safety | Virtual spaces

Moderator Workflows for Virtual Communities

Designed moderation concepts for immersive community spaces, focusing on transparency, escalation, user safety, and preserving the flow of social interaction.

  • Design focus: escalation paths, feedback loops, user-facing transparency.
  • Outcome: informed moderation best practices for emerging platform contexts.
AR | Mobile training

Interactive Bystander Awareness Training

Designed a mobile, geolocation-enabled AR training experience that placed users in interactive scenarios and helped them practice intervention strategies.

  • Design focus: situated learning, scenario flow, consequence feedback.
  • Outcome: recognized at CHI 2023 for outstanding transformative application.
Immersive UX | Safety mechanics

VR Boundary and Consent Mechanics

Designed and evaluated VR-native interaction prompts for preventing interpersonal harm while keeping immersive experiences clear, embodied, and usable.

  • Design focus: timing, user control, boundary setting, continuity.
  • Outcome: published guidance for consent-oriented immersive spaces.
VR | Creator tools

VR Copyright and Creator Attribution System

Designed a VR concept for artists to register, display, and protect digital creations in immersive environments using verifiable ownership cues.

  • Design focus: artist workflows, verification cues, display and discovery.
  • Outcome: user-tested with artists to identify trust and value signals.
Mobile UX | Community platform

Mentorship and Community Platform for Computing Students

Designed a mobile-first platform to help underrepresented computing students find mentors, events, opportunities, and peer support.

  • Design focus: onboarding, event discovery, mentor matching, community trust.
  • Outcome: early user feedback shaped community-support design recommendations.
